01 — about

The person behind the queries

I grew up in Jabalpur, India, and finished my B.Tech in Computer Science there in 2024. That same summer, I packed my life into two suitcases and moved across the world to start my Master's at George Mason University — my first time living outside India.

It was harder than any exam I'd ever taken. New country, new accents, new grading systems, figuring out apartments and groceries and winters alone, all while my family was nine and a half hours ahead on the clock. There were semesters where the only familiar thing in my day was a SQL prompt.

But that's exactly what shaped how I work. Moving countries teaches you the same things good engineering does: plan for what can go wrong, ask for help earlier than your ego wants to, and keep showing up when things break. I graduated with a 3.73 GPA, led a six-person capstone team, and landed in Seattle — a long way from Jabalpur, and I'm just getting started.
